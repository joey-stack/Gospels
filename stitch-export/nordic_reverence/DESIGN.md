## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies a contemporary Scandinavian worship aesthetic: warm, sacred, light-filled, and quietly architectural. Rooted in Gothenburg's gospel music community, the design fuses modern Nordic restraint with deep spiritual joy, warmth, and accessibility. It deliberately eschews cold corporate tech tropes and heavy traditional liturgical ornamentation in favor of generous light, serene typographic pacing, and intentional spiritual grounding.

The interface balances reverence with modern communal invitation. High-key light-filled surfaces contrast deliberately with profound charcoal sanctuaries for focused music playback and reflective scripture intake. Subtle atmospheric warmth, generous negative space, and disciplined tactile card components evoke an open cathedral acoustic—airy, clean, resonant, and welcoming to all.

## Colors

The color system is calibrated for warmth and contemplation without harsh optical extremes:

- **Primary Accent (`#0D9488` / `#14B8A6` / `#0F766E`):** Tech Turquoise acts as the lifeblood and navigational compass. It is applied selectively to primary calls to action, active navigation tabs, event date badges, choir section highlights, and audio play states.
- **Canvas & Surfaces (`#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9`):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) is prohibited for full-canvas backdrops. The base background utilizes warm pale slate `#F8FAFC`, creating a gentle, non-glaring parchment warmth under Swedish daylight.
- **Dark Containers & Sacred Sections (`#0F172A` and `#1E293B`):** Deep rich charcoal provides grounded acoustic focus for evening service features, audio streaming players, and immersive event programs. Pure black (`#000000`) is never used.
- **Text Hierarchy:** Titles and display text use `#111827` for deep, soft contrast. Body and secondary copy utilize refined slates (`#4B5563` and `#64748B`), preserving legibility with gentle optical weight.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Subtle, low-opacity slate lines (`#E2E8F0` at 100% or `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`) frame containers cleanly without breaking spatial flow.

## Typography

The typographic balance relies on a dual-engine hierarchy:
1. **Primary Workhorse (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its welcoming, modern Scandinavian clarity. It handles display, navigation, UI controls, schedules, and structural body copy with warmth and tight geometric rhythm.
2. **Sacred Voice (EB Garamond Italic):** Reserved specifically for biblical scriptures, choral lyrics, song quotes, and spiritual reflections. Rendered exclusively in italicized, medium-contrast serif sizing, it provides an immediate visual shift into reverence and meditative reflection.

All uppercase metadata labels use generous tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.08em`) to guarantee legibility against muted charcoal and off-white backdrops.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured on an adaptable 12-column grid on desktop (max container width 1240px, centered with fluid side margins), transitioning to a 6-column grid on tablet (768px–1023px) and a single-column 4-grid unit on mobile (<768px).

Spatial pacing prioritizes air and unhurried composition. Section transitions employ generous vertical gaps (`space-2xl` to `6rem`) to let music releases, choir tour dates, and devotional themes breathe. Horizontal padding across cards and content panels scales between `space-md` (mobile) and `space-xl` (desktop), maintaining structural alignment with grid lines.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of ambient tinted diffusion and crisp surface definition:

- **Ambient Tinted Shadows:** Shadows avoid murky grey-black. They are cast using translucent slate tinted with subtle cool cyan: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.04)`.
- **Card Framing:** Elevated cards on light backgrounds combine this diffused shadow with a razor-thin border: `1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)` or `#E2E8F0`. This anchors the element with crisp Nordic precision.
- **Deep Charcoal Layers:** In dark surfaces (`#0F172A`), elevation is achieved through tonal stacking rather than heavy shadows: inner cards use `#1E293B`, paired with a delicate interior border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Modal & Floating Elements:** Floating music players and modal dialogues utilize soft elevation: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)` alongside a subtle backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a disciplined **12px (`0.75rem`) border radius** across all primary cards, modules, and container panels. This hits the sweet spot between architectural discipline and welcoming warmth.

- **Cards & Content Panels:** Fixed at 12px (`0.75rem`) corner rounding.
- **Buttons, Badges, & Chips:** Micro-elements use a softer 8px (`0.5rem`) radius, while floating action pills and audio play buttons employ complete circular pill radius (`9999px`).
- **Inner Nested Media:** Images and video embeds nested inside cards mirror the 8px inner corner radius to retain concentric harmony with the parent 12px container.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Tech Turquoise (`#0D9488`), text in `#FFFFFF`, font-weight 600, 8px rounded corners. Hover transitions to `#0F766E` with subtle ambient turquoise glow.
- **Secondary / Subtle:** Outlined in `1.5px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.15)` with text in `#0F172A`. On charcoal backgrounds: `1.5px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` with text in `#F8FAFC`.
- **Icon / Player Buttons:** Circular (`rounded-full`), `#0D9488` background for play/pause triggers with white icons.

### Bespoke Date Badges
Designed specifically for choir rehearsals, worship nights, and Gothenburg concert schedules:
- Compact two-tier vertical badge positioned on the top-left of event cards.
- Upper header: Tech Turquoise background (`#0D9488`) with the month abbreviated in bold uppercase white text (`label-sm`).
- Lower body: Warm off-white (`#F8FAFC`) with the date numeral rendered in deep charcoal (`#0F172A`) in bold headline-sm typography.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.2)` bounding the entire badge with a 6px corner radius.

### Cards
- Surface background set to `#FFFFFF` on `#F8FAFC` canvas, or `#1E293B` within dark sections.
- Rounded strictly to 12px with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0` on light; `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` on dark).
- Generous internal padding: 24px (`space-lg`).

### Scripture & Meditation Blocks
- Callout containers framed by a left-hand 3px solid Tech Turquoise border.
- Background uses subtle off-white parchment tint `#F1F5F9`.
- Quotes styled in `EB Garamond` italic with `#111827`, accompanied by citation text underneath in uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`label-sm`, `#64748B`).

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #CBD5E1`, 8px border radius, 12px 16px internal padding.
- Focused state: Border changes to `#0D9488` with a soft 3px box-shadow ring in `rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.15)`.
- Placeholder text: Slate `#94A3B8`.

### Chips & Ministry Filters
- Minimal, pill-shaped tags used for gospel repertoire categorization (e.g., "Worship Night", "Youth Choir", "Concert", "Workshop").
- Inactive: `#F1F5F9` background with `#4B5563` text.
- Active: Tech Turquoise `#0D9488` with `#FFFFFF` text.
