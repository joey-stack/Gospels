The Four Pillars of Gothenburg Worship Unity

replace_video 1. Christ-Centered Reverence

All choral and liturgical musical repertoire elevates Jesus Christ alone, rooted firmly in scripture and historic apostolic faith.

diversity_3 2. Trans-denominational Unity

We lay aside denominational pride, acknowledging the Holy Spirit's diverse expressions across Gothenburg's churches.

balance 3. Theological & Financial Integrity

Transparent open-book accounting. Zero speaker honorarium inflation; non-profit stewardship audited yearly.

meeting_room 4. Free Public Admission

City-wide worship nights and concert halls must remain freely accessible to anyone seeking Christ, without financial barrier.

security Cryptographically secured under Swedish eIDAS & BankID infrastructure

Sanctuary Network Benefits

Affiliated parishes enjoy direct pastoral and liturgical resources backed by the regional trust:

  • check_circle Shared Multitrack Library: Full stems and choral vocal guides for Nordic gospel anthems.
  • check_circle CCLI Group Clearance: Pre-cleared arrangements and localized Swedish psalm translations.
  • check_circle Choral Masterclasses: Free quarterly seminars in vocal conditioning and sacred polyphony.
  • check_circle Equipment Loan Pool: Access to emergency wireless racks, microphones, and digital mixers.
