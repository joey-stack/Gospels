Clarity & Confidence

Stewardship Questions Answered

help_outline Is GOSPELS an officially registered Swedish non-profit (Ideell förening)? expand_more
Yes. GOSPELS is registered as an ideell förening under organization number 802490-1234 in Gothenburg, Västra Götaland. We operate under Swedish non-profit governance regulations with independent annual auditing presented at our yearly general meeting.
help_outline Are tickets or admissions ever charged for GOSPELS events? expand_more
Never. It is a foundational pillar of our vision that every gathering, concert, and worship session—including Holy Spirit Worship and Göteborgs Lovsångskonsert—is 100% free of charge to all citizens and visitors, funded solely through voluntary kingdom giving.
help_outline Can I deduct my donation on Swedish taxes (Skattereduktion för gåvor)? expand_more
Giving towards our registered social relief initiatives meets the criteria established under Skatteverket's regulations for gifts to approved organizations (minimum 200 SEK per gift, up to 12,000 SEK annually). If you supply your personnummer with your monthly partnership, your Swedish tax deduction is reported automatically.
help_outline How do I update or cancel my monthly Kingdom Partner giving? expand_more
You maintain full sovereignty over your partnership. Every monthly receipt email contains a one-click direct management link allowing you to adjust tiers, update your bank account or card, or pause contributions instantly. You may also email info@gospels.se at any time.
