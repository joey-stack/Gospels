Worship Beyond Church Walls

For generations, Gothenburg’s vibrant gospel tradition has filled church sanctuaries and recital halls. In the late summer of 2027, GOSPELS invites every believer, choir, and seeker to step outdoors onto the historic cobblestones of Gustav Adolfs Torg.

This gathering breaks denominational divisions, welcoming traditional Lutheran parishes, Pentecostal choirs, diaspora fellowships, and indie worship teams into one united voice. Our mandate is simple: to offer a sacrificial fragrance of joy and praise to bless our city, its leaders, its transit commuters, and its youth.

Who Is This Concert For?

The cathedral of Gothenburg is its public square. We prepare space with intentional care for three vital groups.

diversity_1

1. Believers & Parishes

Uniting all Christian denominations—from Swedish Church parishes and Frikyrkor to immigrant congregations—in public adoration, communal intercession, and harmonious praise.

2. Seekers & Commuters

Encountering grace, hope, and sacred song in everyday city life. Whether walking between trams or pausing on the square, you are invited to listen without expectation or pressure.
